Trinity Tally handwritten-digit reader

The bundled weights in digits.bin were trained for this app on the MNIST training set of handwritten numerals (Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes and Christopher J. C. Burges). The classifier has a 16 × 16 input, two dense hidden layers and ten digit outputs. The browser returns tentative ranks for digits 1 to 5 and flags other or uncertain marks for review. The recogniser never enforces a permutation or uses the other ranks to guess an unclear mark.

Training data source: https://storage.googleapis.com/cvdf-datasets/mnist/
MNIST dataset reference: https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/mnist
